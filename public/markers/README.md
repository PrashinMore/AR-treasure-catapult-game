# AR Marker Setup

## Creating Your MindAR Marker

1. **Visit the MindAR Marker Generator:**
   - Go to: https://hiukim.github.io/mind-ar-js-doc/tools/compile/
   - Or use: https://hiukim.github.io/mind-ar-js-doc/tools/designer/

2. **Design Your Marker:**
   - Upload your café logo or design
   - Ensure high contrast (black/white works best)
   - Recommended size: 512x512px or larger
   - Keep it simple for better tracking

3. **Download the Marker:**
   - Click "Compile" to generate the `.mind` file
   - Save it as `cafe-marker.mind` in this directory

4. **Print Your Marker:**
   - Print on high-quality paper or cardstock
   - Minimum size: 8x8 inches (20x20 cm)
   - Ensure good contrast and no glare
   - Laminate for durability

## Alternative: Using a Simple Pattern

If you don't have a logo, you can:
- Use a QR code-style pattern
- Create a geometric design
- Use text with high contrast

## Testing Your Marker

1. Place the marker on a flat surface
2. Open the app and point camera at marker
3. Ensure good lighting
4. Keep marker in view and flat

## Troubleshooting

- **Marker not detected:** Try better lighting or larger print
- **Tracking unstable:** Ensure marker is flat and well-lit
- **Poor performance:** Use a simpler marker design
